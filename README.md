# FORME - Luxury Botanical Self-Care Ecommerce Website

**Not a routine. A ritual.**

---

## Overview

FORME is a modern luxury botanical self-care ecommerce website designed with minimalism, elegance, and intentionality at its core.

The brand features:
- **Rose Water** - Pure & Steam Distilled Facial Mist
- **Rosemary Water** - Pure & Steam Distilled Facial Mist
- **The Ritual Duo** - Complete collection offering

---

## Design Philosophy

### Brand Identity
- **Tagline**: "Not a routine. A ritual."
- **Aesthetic**: Luxury apothecary meets modern minimalism
- **Feel**: Calm, intentional, soft, clean, premium

### Design System

#### Color Palette
- **Cream**: `#faf7f2` - Primary background
- **Off-White**: `#f5f0eb` - Secondary background
- **Beige**: `#e8dcc8` - Accent
- **Warm Beige**: `#d4c4b0` - Depth
- **Kraft Brown**: `#8b7355` - Warm accents
- **Dusty Rose**: `#a8857c` - Emotional accent
- **Sage Green**: `#a8b8a8` - Botanical accent
- **Black**: `#1a1a1a` - Typography & contrast

#### Typography
- **Serif**: Lora (headings, logo, elegant text)
- **Sans-Serif**: Raleway (body, navigation)

#### Spacing
- Generous whitespace throughout
- Balanced padding and margins
- Mobile-first responsive design

## Branding Implementation

The FORME brand is consistently reinforced throughout:

1. **Logo**: Elegant serif typography with underline detail
   - Black on light backgrounds
   - Cream on dark backgrounds

2. **Watermarks**: Subtle FORME watermarks on product cards and imagery

3. **Brand Language**: "Rituals" instead of "products," "Add to Ritual" instead of "Add to Cart"

4. **Visual Hierarchy**: Logo visible in navigation, hero, story, and footer

5. **Emotional Messaging**: Every section reinforces "Not a routine. A ritual."

## Getting Started

1. Clone the repository
2. Open `index.html` in a web browser
3. Customize product images and content as needed
4. Deploy to your hosting service

---

## Customization Guide

### Product Images
Replace placeholder images in the `.product-image` divs with actual product photos.

### Content Updates
- Edit product descriptions in the product cards
- Update testimonials in the testimonials section
- Modify footer links to your actual pages

### Color Adjustments
All colors are defined in CSS variables at the root level for easy customization.
